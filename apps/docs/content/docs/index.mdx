---
title: whatsappd
description: A typed WhatsApp Session with a durable Current Mirror and synchronized WhatsApp Client.
---

whatsappd gives a Node.js application a typed WhatsApp Session, a durable Current Mirror, and a synchronized WhatsApp Client for chats, contacts, groups, messages, and WhatsApp Operations.
